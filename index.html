The problem

Online reviews are broken.

Anyone can write a review. Anywhere. Without ever buying anything. Your customers know it, and they have stopped trusting reviews altogether.

Fakes are cheap and everywhere

Reviews can be bought by the dozen, and bought ones look exactly like real ones. You compete against them with nothing but your word.

Your reviews live on someone else's site

The platform owns the page and sets the rules. Your reputation lives on their terms, not yours.

Even real reviews look fake

Without proof, five stars is just a claim. Buyers are skeptical by default, and that skepticism costs you the sale.

What verified means

Verified means a real payment happened.

No badge to take on faith. Three things have to be true, and you can check all three yourself.

01 · The payment

Somebody actually paid

Every review is tied to a real charge in your Stripe account: an amount, a date, a receipt. No payment, no review.

02 · The buyer

Only that buyer can answer

The invitation goes to the address on the payment. Nobody else can write on that customer's behalf, and nobody can write twice.

03 · The link

Anyone can open it

Each review carries a public link that shows the payment behind it. You, a customer, a stranger: no account, no login.

We can read your payments. We can never change them.

It's not trust by promise. It's trust by proof.
